# The BeCopenhagen app: a quick guide

Welcome! This is the app we use to run tours and the rental shop day to day. You have access to both sides, since you will be guiding and working the shop. Here is what everything does.

## Getting in

Go to **fleet.interestingtours.dk** on your phone (you can add it to your home screen so it feels like a normal app). Log in with your email and the password you set up. If you ever forget it, tap "Set up / forgot password" and you will get a new link by email.

## Two views: Guide and Shop

Because you do both jobs, you have a small **view switcher** near the top. Tap it to move between:

- **Guide view**, for when you are leading tours
- **Shop view**, for when you are working the rental shop

They show different things because the two jobs need different tools. Switch to whichever matches what you are doing that day. Nothing you do in one breaks the other.

## Guide view: what each tab does

- **Action** is where you check bikes out to a customer or take them back in. You pick the bikes, note who they are for, and confirm.
- **Tours** shows your upcoming tours: the date, time, tour type, how many guests, and which bikes are set aside. This is where you confirm what is on your plate for the days ahead. Private tours only appear once they have a booking.
- **Profile** is your own details and settings.
- **Log** is a running list of everything that has happened with the bikes. If something was recorded wrong, you can tap an entry to correct it.

When a tour gets assigned to you, you also get an email, so you do not have to keep checking the app.

## Shop view: what each tab does

- **Today** is the shop's morning picture: how many bikes of each type you need ready today, the day's tour departures and rental pickups, and which bikes are due back. Start here.
- **Action** is the same check out / check in tool as in the guide view, for rentals and walk ins.
- **Repairs** is where bikes that need fixing get logged and tracked.
- **Tours** shows the day's tours, so you know what the shop needs to prepare.
- **Rentals** is the list of rental bookings, with the ability to check bikes out and back in per booking.
- **Bikes** shows the live status of every bike (available, out, in repair) under "Status", and the full bike catalogue under "Fleet" if you ever need to edit bike details.
- **Log** is the same everything list as in the guide view, tap to correct.

## A few things worth knowing

- **The numbers come from FareHarbor.** Tours, bookings, and who is assigned all flow in automatically, so the app stays in step with the booking system. You do not enter tours by hand.
- **"Bikes needed today"** on the Today board is the total to have ready across all of the day's tours and rentals, counted so that bikes shared between non overlapping tours are not double counted. It tells you what to prep, not what is already out.
- **Guests, not bookings.** Counts show the number of people, so a group of eleven on one booking shows as eleven.
- **If something looks wrong**, there is a "Report a bug" button at the top, and you can always ask Federico.

That is the whole thing. It is built to be quick on a phone while you are busy, so most of it is a tap or two. The best way to learn it is to open it and poke around, you cannot break anything by looking.
